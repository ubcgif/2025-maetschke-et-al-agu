## Joint Inversion of Magnetotelluric and P-Wave Travel Time Data at Mount St. Helens Volcano Using a Variation of Information Constraint

**Authors:** 
Katharina Maetschke<sup>1</sup>, Max Moorkamp<sup>2</sup>, Graham Hill<sup>3</sup>, Lindsey
Heagy<sup>1</sup>

1) University of British Columbia, Department of Earth, Ocean and
Atmospheric Sciences, Vancouver, BC, Canada\
2) Technical University Berlin, Faculty VI, Applied Geophysics,
Berlin, Germany\
3) Czech Academy of Sciences, Institute of Geophysics, Prague, Czech
Republic

**Venue:** [American Geophysical Union Annual Meeting 2025](https://www.agu.org/annual-meeting-2025)

### Abstract
We present a first case study applying a Variation of Information (VI)
coupling constraint in the joint inversion of magnetotelluric (MT) and
P-wave travel time data in a volcanological setting. Our study utilizes
previously published data sets from Mount St. Helens, USA, which were
individually inverted. By combining two geophysical methods that are
both sensitive to the presence of melt and hydrothermal fluids, we
demonstrate that the VI coupling effectively reduces ambiguities in the
interpretation of anomalies. Additionally, it permits obtaining
parameter relationships without any a priori assumptions about the
connection between conductivity and seismic velocity. Clustering
analyses of these derived parameter relationships provide new insight
into the spatial distribution of features characterized by specific
conductivity and P-wave velocity relationships, which enhances
interpretability. Our approach highlights the potential of VI-based
coupling in enhancing the information gain and reducing interpretational
ambiguities when using joint inversions in complex volcanic
environments.
